# Code of Conduct

Accountancy Master is made by and for students, teachers and anyone who wants to help them learn. Everyone taking part
in its issues, discussions and pull requests is expected to help keep it a welcoming place.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners and with people writing in a second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain your reasons and listen to theirs.
- Give credit, and only contribute what you have the right to share.
- Keep to the topic: learning and teaching Accountancy, and making this app better.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information, such as a phone number, address or email, without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or posting copied exam papers, books or other material that is not yours to share.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or discussion (in its **…** menu), and send it to the
maintainers of this repository. Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled in confidence.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, discussions and pull requests that break this code, and may
block anyone from taking part, for a time or for good.

This code takes its ideas from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
